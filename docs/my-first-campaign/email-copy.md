# Email copy — Campaign 1: Active UK Meta advertisers

Written to the `/campaign-copywriting` rules: 50–90 words, no em dashes, no banned tokens from `/spam-word-checker` (including “marketing”, “get”, “new”, “now”, “deal”, “performance”, “diagnostics”). CTA is a one-word reply.

Replace `YOUR_UK_ADDRESS` and the privacy URL before upload.

Smartlead/Instantly variables use `{{company_name}}` not `{{company}}`.

---

## Step 1 direction (locked)

- **Audience:** Founder or ecommerce lead at a UK Ltd already running Meta ads.
- **Pain:** Spend is live, but the account is messy or unmeasured.
- **Offer:** Three specific notes in 15 minutes. They keep the notes either way.
- **Proof:** Your years in agency paid media. Do not name confidential client ROAS in cold email. Public-safe line only.
- **Variables:** `{{first_name}}`, `{{company_name}}`, `{{product_hook}}`, `{{ad_example}}`

---

## Email 2 (day 3–4) — thread, empty subject

Rotate to “make money” (more from the same spend). Do not start with “just following up”.

```
{{first_name}}, most founder-led shops I see are paying for ads that cannot be tied back to a basket.

If that is {{company_name}}, the 15-minute pass is me pointing at three fixes. No software to buy.

Is that on your radar?
```

(Same footer as email 1.)

## Email 3 (day 7–8) — new thread

**Subject:** question for {{first_name}}

```
{{first_name}}, when you look at {{company_name}}'s Meta account, can you say which ad made money last week?

If the answer is messy, I will do a 15-minute pass and send three notes. Used to run this inside WPP and Publicis buying teams.

Worth a look?
```

---

## Email 4 (day 11–12) — last note

```
{{first_name}}, last note from me.

If a 15-minute pass on the live ads is not useful, say stop and I will not write again.

If it is, reply Y and I will send the three notes after we talk.
```

---

## After they reply Y

Same hour, not the next day:

1. Send a 15-minute calendar link.
2. Before the call: open their Ad Library ads + homepage. Write a private 3-bullet draft so you are not thinking on the spot.
3. On the call: share the three notes. Ask if they want help implementing. If no, leave the notes. That is the magnet working.
