# UK GDPR legitimate interests — paid ads outreach
Operator: Ejaz Ahmed / Data with Ejaz
Date: 2026-08-15
This is an internal note, not legal advice. Keep it with the campaign files.

## Purpose
Contact people in a professional role at UK limited companies / LLPs to offer a 15-minute paid-ads diagnostic relevant to that role.

## Necessity
The people who can buy this work are not reachable at scale by waiting for inbound only. Email to a published or reasonably inferred work address is a proportionate way to make a relevant professional offer.

## Balancing
- Data used: name, job title, company, work email, public ad creative, public website. No special category data.
- Recipients are at **corporate subscribers** (Ltd / LLP). Sole traders and personal Gmail addresses are excluded.
- Content is about their live advertising, not a consumer product.
- Identity is real. Privacy policy is linked. Opt-out is in every email and honoured the same day.
- Volume is low (two inboxes, capped daily sends).

## Outcome
Legitimate interests can be relied on for this campaign if the filters above are kept. If a recipient objects, suppress them on every future list.

## Operational rules
1. Company-domain emails only.
2. Unsubscribe / “stop” honoured immediately across all sequences.
3. Physical UK address in the footer.
4. No purchased “lead dumps”.
5. Privacy page live before first send.
